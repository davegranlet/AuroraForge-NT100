## What is Aurora Forge NT100 (100% Not Tribute)?

It is my answer to being told that my money and opinion didn't mean s**t to the developers of other modding tools.

People (myself included) have poured years of subscription money into tools that then punish them for a single missed payment: features that were already downloaded get deliberately locked, multi-file extraction gets cut down to one file at a time, and years of continuous support suddenly mean nothing.

I was working on my stupid prompt builders, not bugging anyone, when I needed a fresh extraction. I loaded up CakeView and... wtf, multi-file extraction is a Patreon feature? I asked about it and was told "Meh." So yeah, nah.

The answer became obvious: build the free alternative and automate the entire pipeline so those gates stop mattering. Every format I map, every offset I solve, and every workflow I automate is one less gate anyone can use to squeeze the community.

The goal is a full toolkit that is free, complete, and stays complete.

## Who am I?

I have been modding since the early PC wrestling days. My roots are the original MDickie modding scene, where I wrote the first PC wrestling game mod for an MDickie title. I code across just about every stack imaginable, from low-level Assembly to high-level automation pipelines. If a computer speaks it, I have built with it.

My philosophy: tear down the barriers to asset extraction. I do not believe in paywalling downloaded software, cutting off features, or exploiting creators. Every time I automate a tool or crack a new file format, it is one less gate anyone can use to squeeze the community.

Active projects: automating the complete PC and console wrestling game asset pipelines, and multi-file parallel extraction tools with zero feature gates. Outside the dev environment my time goes into wrestling, MMA, and deep strategy gaming.

One note: I am pretty chill, but if I come across as blunt or quiet during active dev hours, that is just deep focus in the structural math - it is not personal.

I am taking the scene back. Let's build.

## What's in 2.1 Beta 4?

- Free: no Patreon requirement, Discord role check, access gate, or in-app verifier.
- Full 2K19 to 2K20 / 2K22 / 2K23 / 2K24 / 2K25 / 2K26 animation conversion matrix.
- CAK Foundry, Entry Studio, Audio Studio, PAC19, CAK20, DDS, Character Viewer, Mod Suite, loaders, and the full tool/source library.
- Public source downloads for the main app, each major tool, each loader, and the one-off utilities.
- Windows short-path fix: extract/copy no longer hits the path limit.
- Tools are 100% compatible with Tribute's BakeMe folders.
- Plugin coverage and support for all PWM/Tribute plugins is coming.
- Tutorials are coming, but the app is very easy to use.

## Patreon

Major releases such as today's release are 100% free, but any further updates and fixes are Patreon-only until our next major release.
