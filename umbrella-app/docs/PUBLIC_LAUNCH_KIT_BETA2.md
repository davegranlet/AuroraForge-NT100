# AuroraForge NT100 Beta2 Public Launch Kit

This is the public-facing campaign kit for the AuroraForge NT100 Beta2 launch. The voice is blunt, human, and pissed off. Keep it punchy. Attack the locked-down business model and describe our experience plainly. Do not use fake accounts, spam communities, or invent claims.

## Core Position

AuroraForge NT100 started with a pretty simple question: why are we still paying every month just to do basic modding? So we started building the tools we actually wanted. Beta2 puts the archive tools, loaders, converters, creator studios, documentation, and research work in one app while we finish the editors.

## Why We Are Building This

> This whole workspace came out of our experience with the Tribute and PWM ecosystem associated with Robert, Fred, and Rippz. We got tired of the same community paying year after year for workflows that should be easier, faster, and more capable.
>
> Two $6 subscriptions. $12 every month. One expires and the workflow is screwed. A lot of the work is moving assets between games and model contexts. We are building AuroraForge NT100 to do that with fewer clicks, better editors, and the features users actually want.
>
> We are not targeting Meta or Discovery. Never used them. Not our fight. This is about the Tribute/PWM access model and the gates we experienced there.

This is our experience and our opinion. Back it with screenshots, receipts, tests, and working demos when the argument gets specific.

The simple version:

> Stop renting your tools. Enter the Forge.

Identity line:

> AuroraForge NT100. One app, our tools, fewer gates, better flow, editors finishing now.

If someone asks what the difference is:

> The current Tribute and PWM workflow requires two separate $6 monthly subscriptions for the meaningful modding capabilities: $6 for Tribute plus $6 for PWM, or $12 every month. Based on our testing, when either subscription expires, the related workflow becomes unavailable or heavily restricted. AuroraForge NT100 is being built as another path: $3 total during active development, with the goal of a free version of the full toolkit and the features users actually want.

The honest access explanation:

> Current builds are Patreon-verified through our $3 total tier during active development. We are working toward a free version of the full toolkit, including the features users actually want, without putting core porting workflows behind a $50 tier. Completed major updates and new major versions are released free to the wider community.

> We have also put out 350 free Patreon memberships through Discord, Reddit, forums, and the Patreon page. The free-sub link is on the Patreon front page. Please look there before asking us to find one for you.

Current build status:

> The core AuroraForge NT100 system is in place. We are finishing the editors that turn the integrated capabilities into complete end-to-end creator workflows.
