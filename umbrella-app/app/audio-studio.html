Audio Studio — Custom Music

Import audio, assign it to a custom music slot, edit the title and star shown in the game picker, then build the package, the MusicList CAK, and the mods manifest.

Folders
Build copies the bundled MusicList.jsfb into your work folder, appends your custom rows there, and bakes the edited copy into CustomMusic.cak in your mods folder. Later builds read that CAK back so added and edited tracks accumulate. Nothing is written to the app files.
