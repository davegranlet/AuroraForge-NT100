AuroraForge NT100 is a free WWE PC modding workspace. It inspects and rebuilds game archives, converts WWE 2K19 motion into new 2K25/2K26 entries, converts textures, prepares the loader, and builds technically exact prompts for the creative tool you choose. What is AuroraForge NT100?

AuroraForge NT100 is a free, all-in-one WWE PC modding workspace. It covers the pipeline that used to be scattered across separate paid programs: CAK extraction, browsing, rebuilding, baking, and verification across WWE 2K20 through 2K26 (with 2K19 PAC support); conversion of WWE 2K19 entrance and victory motion into brand-new 2K25/2K26 entries with a loader-ready CAK; DDS texture conversion; loader setup, backup, and rollback; and guided prompt builders for faces, masks, tattoos, attire, logos, belts, arenas, and complete characters.

Why was AuroraForge NT100 made?

As a direct response to anti-consumer modding software. People have poured years of subscription money into tools that then punish a single missed payment: already-downloaded features get locked, multi-file extraction gets cut down to one file at a time, and years of continuous support suddenly mean nothing. AuroraForge NT100 is the answer to that: a free toolkit that automates the pipeline and never gates features. Every format we map and every workflow we automate is one less gate anyone can use to squeeze the community.

Where is AuroraForge NT100 going?

Toward a fully automated WWE asset pipeline across PC (and console where the platform allows it): extraction, conversion, packaging, and in-game validation in one flow. True parallel multi-file extraction with zero feature gates. A finished converter with in-game alignment and facial conversion. Every completed major update and new major version released free to the wider community. And full open source once the extraction and encryption work is cleared for release.

Who builds AuroraForge NT100?

A modding veteran from the early PC wrestling days, with roots in the original MDickie modding scene (including the first PC wrestling game mod for an MDickie title). The philosophy is simple: tear down the barriers to asset extraction — no paywalling downloaded software, no cutting off features, no exploiting creators. If it comes across as blunt or quiet during active dev hours, that is deep focus in the structural math, not personal. We are taking the scene back. Let's build.

Is AuroraForge NT100 an AI chatbot?

No. AuroraForge NT100 is not a conversational assistant and does not answer a prompt as a chatbot would. It prepares the instructions and supporting files that the user takes to a compatible AI or image-generation tool.

Does AuroraForge NT100 generate the finished artwork?

No. AuroraForge NT100 generates the prompt and handoff materials. The external tool chosen by the user generates the image or other creative result.

What does AuroraForge NT100 actually generate?

Depending on the selected builder, AuroraForge NT100 can produce a finished prompt, project brief, checklist, profile JSON, layout references, expected-output notes, and a ZIP handoff pack containing the files needed for the next step.

Why not type a short request directly into an AI tool?

WWE texture work has technical constraints that are easy to omit. AuroraForge NT100 helps specify the target, layout, dimensions, materials, required files, preservation rules, things to avoid, and validation steps so the external tool receives a clearer request.

Do I need to understand prompt engineering?

No. The builders use normal project questions and domain-specific choices. AuroraForge NT100 assembles those choices into detailed instructions automatically.

What is a handoff pack?

A handoff pack is a ZIP containing the finished prompt and the supporting technical files required by that workflow. Review its contents, then upload the relevant files to the compatible AI or image tool you choose.

Is AuroraForge NT100 tied to ChatGPT or one AI provider?

No. AuroraForge NT100 does not send prompts automatically and is not tied to a single provider. Compatibility depends on whether the chosen tool can accept the prompt and required reference files.

Can I review, copy, or export the prompt?

Yes. Builders provide prompt or handoff exports, and applicable workflows provide copy or download controls. Review the instructions and project choices before using them externally.

What is Aurora CAK Foundry?

Aurora CAK Foundry is the extractor, browser, rebuilder, baker, and verifier. It reads original CAKs without changing them and writes a separate protected mod CAK from the contents of a selected BakeMe folder. It supports WWE 2K20 through 2K26 with automatic version detection (FDIR 9.3 and 9.9 are handled natively, and 2K19 archives open through the PAC tools). Aurora derives the 2K26 v9.9 key from the final filename: do not rename a finished CAK; rebuild it under the desired name.

What is the Entry Studio?

The Entry Studio runs inside the app (no browser and no local server). It creates a new Create-an-Entrance entry, converts a WWE 2K19 motion into the 2K25/2K26 clips format, lets you edit the entry fields, bakes a loader-ready CAK, and stages it. The registration writers preserve the existing game tables byte-for-byte and only append the new rows.

Does the converter need my own game files?

Yes, and it always uses your own files. Point the wizard at your WWE 2K19 install and your own WWE 2K25/2K26 install. The registration skeleton is rebuilt from your own game on your PC, and the output CAK is written to your Documents folder. Nothing from anyone else's machine is used, and no developer paths ship in the app.

Did AI invent DataCtrlLink?

No. DataCtrlLink was already a working, human-created plugin before AI became involved. The developer asked AI to audit and help secure their own existing work.

Why did the audit begin with compiled code?

The released compiled plugin represented what an ordinary user or attacker could inspect. Providing the original source first would have defeated that security test. Tests continue to validate compiled programs and packaged outputs; source review alone is not treated as proof.

Was third-party software examined?

Yes. Compiled software and observable behavior were examined for interoperability research. No third-party source code was available, copied, translated, or included. This is not described as a formal clean-room process. It is an independent, security-focused interoperability implementation based on the developer's own original work.

Does Secure DataCtrlLink support every WWE 2K26 release?

No. AuroraForge NT100 fails closed and accepts only explicitly reviewed executable and DLL checksums. Setup includes backup, rollback, diagnostics, and experimental manifest/collision controls for the reviewed profile; expanding to more releases remains future work.

Does AuroraForge NT100 send my project to an AI automatically?

No. AuroraForge NT100 prepares files locally. The user controls if, when, and where a prompt or handoff pack is uploaded.

What are the other tools for?

Project, inspection, preview, conversion, setup, loader, and tutorial tools support the full modding workflow. They organize, convert, or validate the work; they do not turn AuroraForge NT100 into a chatbot and do not replace the external creative-generation step.

Who is AuroraForge NT100 for?

WWE PC modders and creators: people converting 2K19 motion into modern entries, rebuilding CAKs, converting textures, preparing the loader, and building clearer prompts with repeatable project organization and a guided path from an idea to a testable in-game result.

Is AuroraForge NT100 open source?

Not yet. The goal is to make AuroraForge NT100 fully open source, but the current public Beta 3 release is compiled-only because the extraction and encryption work cannot be published until the source is cleared. When a cleared source package is available, it will be posted. Required third-party license notices are included with the project.
