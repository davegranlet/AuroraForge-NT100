Open CakeView settings

Before browsing game files, make sure CakeView knows where WWE 2K26 and your working folders are located.

  1. Open Settings.Use the settings button from CakeView's main screen.
  2. Check your folders.Select the WWE 2K26 game directory and your preferred work location.
  3. Save and return.Do not continue until the selected locations look correct.
