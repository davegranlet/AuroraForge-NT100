AuroraForge NT100 Beta 3

A portable WWE PC modding workspace that folds the supporting converter, loader, archive, texture, tutorial, and prompt-builder work into one Beta 3 app experience. Internal build: v2.0.a.

Animation conversion remains a controlled beta. Test replacement output in game and keep a rollback before treating a candidate as proven.

Important boundaries
  • AuroraForge NT100 is not affiliated with or endorsed by WWE, 2K, Visual Concepts, or the maintainers of third-party modding tools.
  • No extracted WWE model, texture, audio, or archive assets are bundled with AuroraForge NT100.
  • Setup does not download or install third-party programs. Users choose software and paths they are authorized to use.
  • Character Viewer is an approximate local preview. It does not reproduce the game renderer and does not write to proprietary files.
  • Back up original files, follow current tool documentation, respect licenses and permissions, and perform final validation in game.
Source availability

AuroraForge NT100 is intended to become fully open source, but the current public Beta 3 release is compiled-only. The extraction and encryption work cannot be published until the source is cleared for release. When a cleared source package is available, it will be posted. The Character Viewer uses Three.js, OBJLoader, and OrbitControls under the MIT License. The DDS Converter includes Microsoft DirectXTex texconv.exe under the MIT License. Required third-party license notices are included with the app.
