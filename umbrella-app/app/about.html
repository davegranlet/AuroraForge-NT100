What this project is

AuroraForge NT100 is a free, all-in-one WWE PC modding toolkit. It covers the parts of the workflow that used to be scattered across separate paid programs: CAK extraction, browsing, rebuilding, and baking across WWE 2K20 through 2K26 (plus 2K19 PAC support); converting WWE 2K19 entrance and victory motion into brand-new 2K20 / 2K22 / 2K23 / 2K24 / 2K25 / 2K26 entries with a loader-ready CAK; DDS texture conversion with original-format matching; loader setup, backup, and rollback; and prompt builders that turn your creative choices into precise instructions for the AI or image tool you choose. No feature gates, no crippled exports, no artificial limits.

Support model: AuroraForge NT100 2.1.7 is free. Optional Patreon subscriptions fund development at patreon.com/c/dgranletmwo. Major releases are free; patch updates and fixes after a major release are Patreon-only until the next major release.

Why I built it

This project is a direct response to anti-consumer modding software. People have poured years of subscription money into tools that then punish them for a single missed payment: features that were already downloaded get deliberately locked, multi-file extraction gets cut down to one file at a time, and years of continuous support suddenly mean nothing. When I saw that happen during active testing of an original extraction tool, the answer became obvious: build the free alternative and automate the entire pipeline so those gates stop mattering.

Every format I map, every offset I solve, and every workflow I automate is one less gate anyone can use to squeeze the community. The goal is a full toolkit that is free, complete, and stays complete.

The man behind the forge

Welcome to the Forge. I have been modding since the early PC wrestling days - my roots are the original MDickie modding scene, where I wrote the first PC wrestling game mod for an MDickie title. I code across just about every stack imaginable, from low-level Assembly to high-level automation pipelines. If a computer speaks it, I have built with it.

My philosophy: tear down the barriers to asset extraction. I do not believe in paywalling downloaded software, cutting off features, or exploiting creators. Every time I automate a tool or crack a new file format, it is one less gate anyone can use to squeeze the community.

Active projects: automating the complete PC and console wrestling game asset pipelines, and multi-file parallel extraction tools with zero feature gates. Outside the dev environment my time goes into wrestling, MMA, and deep strategy gaming.

One note: I am pretty chill, but if I come across as blunt or quiet during active dev hours, that is just deep focus in the structural math - it is not personal. I am taking the scene back. Let's build.

Important boundaries
  • AuroraForge NT100 is not affiliated with or endorsed by WWE, 2K, Visual Concepts, or the maintainers of third-party modding tools.
  • No extracted WWE model, texture, audio, or archive assets are bundled with AuroraForge NT100.
  • Setup does not download or install third-party programs. Users choose software and paths they are authorized to use.
  • Character Viewer is an approximate local preview. It does not reproduce the game renderer and does not write to proprietary files.
  • Back up original files, follow current tool documentation, respect licenses and permissions, and perform final validation in game.
