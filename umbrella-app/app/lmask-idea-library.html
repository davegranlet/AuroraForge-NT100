Idea Editor

Built-in ideas cannot be overwritten. Saving a built-in idea creates a custom editable copy.

How custom ideas are used

After saving a custom idea, it appears in the Design idea dropdowns on Luchador Mask Art, Luchador Mask Studio, and Mapping Handoff. Custom ideas are saved in your browser using localStorage. Export a JSON idea pack when you want a backup.
