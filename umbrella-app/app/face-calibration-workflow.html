What to look for

  • Did the brows or eyes land too high or too low?
  • Does the face feel vertically compressed?
  • Is the forehead or hairline taking up too much space?
  • Does the skin tone clash with the body?
  • Did beard, brows, or pores become too harsh?
  • Is the mouth or chin shifted or compressed?
  • Does the overall face scale look too large or too small?
  • Orientation check: was the final game-test copy flipped vertically before DDS conversion?
