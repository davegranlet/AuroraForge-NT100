Known safe result

What the Slade sample proved

The first tested Slade sample showed a face texture-only replacement: BaseModel/Textures/head_color.dds changed, while MCD, MTLS, JMTL, and YCL files stayed unchanged. That means a read-only inspector can help confirm when a replacement is texture-only before the user tests in game.
