THE HONEST ANSWERS

BETA 4 FAQ.

Short answers, no marketing fog.

Is Beta 4 really free?

Yes. 2.1.3 Beta 4 is free for everyone. No Patreon requirement, Discord role check, access gate, or in-app verifier. Optional Patreon support funds future work.

Do I need Patreon?

No. 2.1.3 is free to use. Patreon is optional; patch updates and fixes after this major release are Patreon-only until the next major release.

What changed?

The verifier is gone, the app runs on the proven 2.0 shell, the full 2K19 to 2K20 / 2K22 / 2K23 / 2K24 / 2K25 / 2K26 matrix is listed, Windows short paths are fixed, and public source is available.

Which games are supported?

CAK and archive tools: 2K20 through 2K26, plus 2K19 PAC. Animation conversion: 2K19 into 2K20, 2K22, 2K23, 2K24, 2K25, or 2K26, plus cross-generation combinations. Loaders: 2K25 and 2K26.

Does the converter need my game files?

Yes. It rebuilds the registration skeleton from your own target game and writes the CAK to your chosen folder. No game data is bundled.

What about the old standalone converter?

It is retired and not shipped because it contains its own Patreon gate. The in-app Animation Converter is the supported path.

Who makes it?

I do. AuroraForge is built by a modder under VikingStudios. It is independent and not affiliated with WWE, 2K, or Visual Concepts.

Where is the source?

Under Source. Each tool, loader, and one-off utility has its own archive, plus an all-source bundle. The public source is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later. Private research, game data, and the private verifier are excluded.

Is it affiliated with WWE or 2K?

No. AuroraForge NT100 is independent modding software and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WWE, 2K, Visual Concepts, or related rights holders.
