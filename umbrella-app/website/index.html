WHY THIS EXISTS

Useful tools. Clear boundaries. Better modding.

Why are we building this? Because the scene turned basic modding into a tool maze with a monthly bill attached. A workflow that breaks the moment one piece updates is a hell of a way to treat a community.

A lot of the work is moving assets from one game or model context to another. We are building AuroraForge NT100 to do that with fewer clicks, better editors, and more useful features. Not a clone. Not another locked box. Our system is built; we are finishing the editors.

Current AuroraForge NT100 access is $3 total while development continues - strictly to support future development. The goal is a free version of the full toolkit, including the features users actually want, with major milestone releases intended to become free public releases. Free subscription links: claim one here.

We want to be fully open source. Some extraction work is not ready for publication because of the encryption-breaking techniques involved. Until it is cleared, protected components remain compiled-only. When cleared source is ready, we will publish it.
