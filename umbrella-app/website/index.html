THE HONEST ANSWERS

FAQ.

Short answers, no marketing fog.

Is AuroraForge NT100 free?

Yes. 2.1.7 Beta 4 is free for everyone. No Patreon requirement, Discord role check, access gate, or in-app verifier. Optional Patreon support funds future work.

Do I need Patreon?

No. 2.1.7 Beta 4 is free to download and use. Patreon is optional; patch updates and fixes after this major release are Patreon-only until the next major release.

What changed in Beta 4?

The verification system is gone, the app runs on the proven 2.0 shell, the animation converter lists the full 2K19 to 2K20 / 2K22 / 2K23 / 2K24 / 2K25 / 2K26 matrix, Windows short paths are fixed, and public source downloads are available.

Which games are supported?

CAK and archive tools cover 2K20 through 2K26 with 2K19 PAC support. Animation conversion covers 2K19 into 2K20, 2K22, 2K23, 2K24, 2K25, or 2K26, plus cross-generation combinations. Loaders cover 2K25 and 2K26.

What does the Animation Converter do?

It refreshes a registration skeleton from your own target game files, converts 2K19 entrance and victory motion into new entries, and bakes a loader-ready CAK. Test the exact output in game and keep a rollback.

Who makes it?

I do. AuroraForge is built by a modder under VikingStudios. It is independent and not affiliated with WWE, 2K, or Visual Concepts.

Is the source available?

Yes. The main app, each major tool, each loader, and the one-off utilities each have a source archive under Source under GPL-3.0-or-later. Private research, game data, and the private verifier are excluded.

Does it work on Linux?

Yes. Windows x64 and Linux x64 builds are provided. Linux defaults to software rendering on machines without a GPU, including VPS and headless hosts.

Is it affiliated with WWE or 2K?

No. AuroraForge NT100 is independent modding software and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WWE, 2K, Visual Concepts, or related rights holders.
